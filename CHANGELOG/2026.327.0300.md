# 2026.327.0300 — feat(ci)!: publish a repair of a shipped version as a new pkgrel, on purpose (successor to #15)

## Summary

**Successor to opencharly/charly-arch#15** (auto-closed after five counting verdicts). Every finding of its rounds is fixed in this body or the diff: the pkgrel is data (`env: RELEASE`, digits-validated, quoted at use), the two dispatch paths are pasted, and — the round that closed it — the `force → skip=false` override is now proven by the **pair**: the same version read `already published: true` and skipped without `force`, then built and published `-2` with it.

A corrected artifact of an already-shipped version must be published as a **new pkgrel**, never by rewriting the published `-1`: a client that already holds `-1` would never see the change. This adds the two inputs that make that explicit, and nothing changes for a normal release.

- **`force: true`** — ignore the "already published" skip; it *requires* `pkgrel`.
- **`pkgrel: <n>`** — validated as digits in `resolve`, carried to the packaging step through `env: RELEASE` (never interpolated into the script text), and quoted at use: `${RELEASE:+--release "$RELEASE"}`.
- With neither input: the same skip, the same names, byte-for-byte today's behaviour.

This is the mechanism `opencharly/layer-charly#23` needed, and it is what actually republished the charly packages as `2026.326.1922-2` with the corrected metadata.
